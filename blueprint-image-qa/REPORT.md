## How it was scored

- Every ChatGPT image was viewed at full size and its labels checked word for word against its prompt or remake prompt. Margins were measured from the pixels: a text tag whose border sits under about 45px from a frame edge is a cramped-margin hard fail (the same bar that failed bp-01 004 v1).
- `check.py`'s cramped and off-brand flags were treated as advisory only.
- Code-drawn rows were scored from `blueprint-smoke/code-drawn/<path>.png` at 61c4f97, viewed at full size with zoomed crops of every tag, leader and chip, and merged by exact (lesson, path). Embedded-still tags that render at about 17-20px at 1080p are an illegible-label hard fail; code tags of about 28px are fine.
