# Audio licence

The beds, the effects, and the ident sting are synthesized by `music/compose.py` and `music/sfx.py`. They are not recordings. They do not include a soundfont, a commercial loop, or a sample from a library.

You can regenerate them from the code. The seed is fixed, so the files match a re-run after the same loudness pass.

The optional ElevenLabs path (`music/eleven.py`) is off unless `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` is set. Nothing in `test-outputs/` came from that API.

Piper speech used beside these files is a stock Piper voice (Lessac medium), downloaded at runtime and not stored in this folder. This licence note does not cover that voice model. The voice skill documents where it comes from.
