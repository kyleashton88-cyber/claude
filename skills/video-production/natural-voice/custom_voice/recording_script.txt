A grid places buy orders below the price.
Sell orders sit above the price.
When price crosses a line, that order fills.
The range is chosen before the start.
Past the range, the grid has left its plan.
A liquidation line sits beyond the range.
The gap between price and that line is the buffer.
If the buffer shrinks, the plan is under stress.
Count the fills.
Read the buffer.
Keep the picture.
Figures in these lessons are illustrative.
This is education, not a forecast.
Pause at the end of each line.
Say the ticker letters clearly when a line includes them.
USDT is spoken as the four letters.
SOL is spoken as sol.
DOGE is spoken as dohj coin.
Read the line once, then stop.
