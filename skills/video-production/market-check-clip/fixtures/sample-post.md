# Market Check

Price spent the week inside a range. That is a description of the path, not an instruction. EXAMPLE the sketch marks 90 and 110. Those marks are a drawing. They are not a forecast.

A market check names what changed. The week stayed between the two marks. A close inside the range is still just a close.

The gap under the range is the buffer. When the buffer shrinks, the plan is under stress. Stress means read the picture again. It is not a call to buy or sell.

Say the high, the low, and the gap. If a number is on the page, it carries the word EXAMPLE. Write those three words down before you leave the page. This is education, not financial advice.
