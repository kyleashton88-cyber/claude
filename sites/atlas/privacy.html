The law we write to

Australia’s Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) sets out the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). The APPs cover, in general terms, open management of personal information, collection, use and disclosure, direct marketing, cross-border disclosure, quality, security, access, and correction.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

The Office of the Australian Information Commissioner publishes the Act, the APPs, and how to make a privacy complaint: https://www.oaic.gov.au/. The Act itself is on the Federal Register of Legislation: https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A03712.

“Personal information” here has the same everyday meaning as in that Act: information or an opinion about an identified person, or a person who is reasonably identifiable.

What this education site is likely to hold

Atlas is a reading library. The current build does not ask you to open a trading account, and it does not ask for a seed phrase, a password to a wallet, or a withdrawal-enabled key. We never ask for seed phrases, passwords, or withdrawal-enabled keys.

We collect:

  • Messages you send. If you email contact@stewartcrowncrypto.com, we receive the address you write from, what you write, and any attachment you include. We use that to reply and to keep a record of the request.
  • Checklist requests. If you ask for The Grid Bot Starter Checklist, we collect your name, email and country so we can send it. The public address of the file is [CHECKLIST LINK] until Stewart publishes it. If you tick the box on the form, we will also send you educational emails about grid bots and our programs. Every email identifies us and has an unsubscribe link, and we action opt-outs within 5 business days.
  • Technical logs. A host typically logs an IP address, browser type, and the page requested, so the site can run and so abuse can be investigated. Our host, [HOST, e.g. Netlify/Vercel/Cloudflare], keeps these logs.
  • On-device progress. Some lessons store a quiz score or a checklist tick in your browser’s local storage. That stays on your device unless a later version clearly says it is sent to a server. You can clear it in your browser.
  • Cookies and third-party connections. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies. When you load a page, your browser also connects to Google Fonts (to load fonts) and to Binance's public data service and CoinGecko (to show market prices). Those providers receive your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies. If we add analytics later, we will update this policy first and, where needed, ask for your consent.
