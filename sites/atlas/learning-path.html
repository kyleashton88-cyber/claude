Beginner checkpoint

A range is useful because it gives the strategy an operating area. The important skill is recognising when that assumption stops being true.

Mastery rule

  • Explain the idea in plain English.
  • Recognise it on a candlestick chart.
  • Calculate the key numbers.
  • State the invalidation condition.
  • Review what happened after the trade or bot run.
