MODULE 09

Futures & Leverage

Understand margin, notional exposure, leverage, funding and liquidation risk before using derivatives.

Leverage changes exposure

Leverage magnifies the notional exposure controlled by margin. It does not improve the quality of the underlying trade idea.

Liquidation is exchange-specific

Maintenance margin, fees, position mode and exchange rules affect liquidation. The simplified sandbox below is educational, not an exchange liquidation calculator.

Funding matters

Perpetual futures can transfer funding between long and short participants. Funding can change over time and should be included in planning.
