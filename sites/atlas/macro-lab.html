Macro & Intermarket Atlas

Learn how rates, currencies, equities and commodities can provide context for risk markets without pretending correlation is permanent.

Rates

Interest-rate expectations influence financing conditions and asset valuation.

US dollar

Dollar strength can interact with global liquidity and risk appetite.

Equities

Technology and crypto can share risk-on/risk-off behaviour at times.

Gold & commodities

Different macro regimes can change cross-asset relationships.

Correlation regimes

Relationships can strengthen, weaken or reverse.

Event risk

Scheduled macro releases can change volatility rapidly.
