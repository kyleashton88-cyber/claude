MODULE 13

Crypto Market Fundamentals

Understand the market infrastructure underneath the chart: liquidity, custody, token categories, networks and crypto-specific risks.

Market liquidity

Depth and spread affect execution.

Custody

Exchange custody and self-custody have different operational risks.

Token categories

Payment, smart-contract, governance, stablecoin and utility designs behave differently.

Networks

Fees, finality and congestion affect transfers.

Stablecoins

Useful settlement assets with issuer, collateral and depeg risks.

Scams & operational risk

Address verification and transaction checks matter because blockchain transfers may be irreversible.
