Risk and position-size calculator

The size is the cash you are willing to lose, divided by the distance to the stop.

Size is the cash at risk divided by the distance from entry to stop. Fees and slippage are basis points on the notional, in and out. The futures cap is compared with notional divided by the account. A venue's margin ladder is not in this form.
