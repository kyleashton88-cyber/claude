Shared review language

Use the same four questions for every setup

01

What is the regime?

Range, trend, breakout or abnormal volatility?

02

What is the idea?

What behaviour are you trying to capture?

03

Where is invalidation?

What tells you the original assumption is wrong?

04

What did the data show?

Review fills, fees, drawdown and the actual outcome.
