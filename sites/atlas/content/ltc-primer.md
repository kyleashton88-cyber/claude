# Litecoin primer

Litecoin is a public proof-of-work blockchain launched as an early alternative to Bitcoin. LTC is the native coin. The design kept the broad idea of a capped issuance schedule and changed two mechanical choices: the hashing algorithm, and a shorter target interval between blocks. This page is general information. It is not a view on the price and not a recommendation to hold LTC.

Faster blocks are not the same thing as faster finality. A payment is still a signed transaction that becomes harder to rewrite as more blocks are built on it. Miners, fees, and the halving-style subsidy are the security budget, as on other proof-of-work chains, and the exact schedule is in the protocol rather than in this primer. Read [Bitcoin](glossary.md#bitcoin) beside [Litecoin](glossary.md#litecoin) if you want the contrast, and the [chain directory](chains.md) if you want the rest of the set. Wallet rules are on the [security page](security.md).
