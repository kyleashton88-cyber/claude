# Litecoin primer

Litecoin is a public layer-1 blockchain launched in 2011 from a copy of the Bitcoin code. Its native coin is LTC. It uses proof of work with the Scrypt hashing function, targets a block about every two and a half minutes, and has a maximum supply of 84 million LTC. The block subsidy halves every 840,000 blocks, roughly every four years. This page is general information. It is not a view on the price and not a recommendation to hold LTC.

Like Bitcoin, Litecoin tracks coins as unspent outputs rather than account balances. An optional extension block, MimbleWimble Extension Blocks, lets users move value with amounts hidden. Many Dogecoin miners also merge-mine Litecoin, which ties the two security budgets together.
