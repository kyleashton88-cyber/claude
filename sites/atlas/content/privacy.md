## The law we write to

Australia’s Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) sets out the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). The APPs cover, in general terms, open management of personal information, collection, use and disclosure, direct marketing, cross-border disclosure, quality, security, access, and correction.

Whether Market Pros Pty Ltd is an APP entity in full depends on facts Stewart must confirm, including turnover and any other test in the Act. This draft does not claim an exemption and does not claim that every APP applies. The site is written so that a small education library can meet those principles in ordinary operation: say what is collected, collect only what the library needs, keep it accurate, protect it, and give people a way to ask for access or correction.

The Office of the Australian Information Commissioner publishes the Act, the APPs, and how to make a privacy complaint: [https://www.oaic.gov.au/](https://www.oaic.gov.au/). The Act itself is on the Federal Register of Legislation: [https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A03712](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A03712).

“Personal information” here has the same everyday meaning as in that Act: information or an opinion about an identified person, or a person who is reasonably identifiable.

## What this education site is likely to hold

Atlas is a reading library. The current build does not ask you to open a trading account, and it does not ask for a seed phrase, a password to a wallet, or a withdrawal-enabled key. We never ask for seed phrases, passwords, or withdrawal-enabled keys.

- **Messages you send.** If you email [CONTACT_EMAIL] or [PRIVACY_CONTACT], we receive the address you write from, what you write, and any attachment you include. We use that to reply and to keep a record of the request.
- **Checklist requests.** If you ask for The Grid Bot Starter Checklist, we collect the details you submit so we can send the file. - **On-device progress.** Some lessons store a quiz score or a checklist tick in your browser’s local storage. That stays on your device unless a later version clearly says it is sent to a server. You can clear it in your browser.
- **Cookies or a similar technology.** If a later version uses analytics or a consent banner, the banner must name the tool and the purpose before it runs. This draft does not turn any tracker on.

We do not ask for government identifiers, health information, or payment card numbers to read the library. ## Why it is collected

Collection is limited to running an education site:

- to send a page, a reply, or The Grid Bot Starter Checklist
- to keep the site available and to investigate misuse
- to fix an error you report
- to meet a record-keeping duty if one applies

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it to build a trading profile of you, and we do not use it to tell you to acquire or dispose of an asset.

Direct marketing, if it is ever used, will follow APP 7 in general terms: a clear choice to receive it, and a way to opt out in each message. This draft does not start a mailing list.

This sentence is general information about that scheme. It is not a statement that a breach has occurred.
