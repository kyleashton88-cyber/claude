## The law we write to

Australia’s Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) sets out the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). The APPs cover, in general terms, open management of personal information, collection, use and disclosure, direct marketing, cross-border disclosure, quality, security, access, and correction.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

The Office of the Australian Information Commissioner publishes the Act, the APPs, and how to make a privacy complaint: [https://www.oaic.gov.au/](https://www.oaic.gov.au/). The Act itself is on the Federal Register of Legislation: [https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A03712](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A03712).

“Personal information” here has the same everyday meaning as in that Act: information or an opinion about an identified person, or a person who is reasonably identifiable.
