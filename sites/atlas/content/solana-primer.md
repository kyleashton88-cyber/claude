# Solana primer

Solana is a public layer-1 blockchain. Its native coin is SOL, which pays fees and is used in staking under the network's proof-of-stake design. The chain is built for high throughput: it aims at short block times and at executing transactions in parallel, rather than only one after another. This page is general information. It is not a view on the price and not a recommendation to hold SOL.

Accounts hold data as well as balances, and programs are deployed accounts that transactions call. Fees are often small beside a congested layer 1, and they are not zero. Outages and how concentrated the validators are belong in any study of a high-throughput chain. A transfer is still a signature. The same seed-phrase rules on the [security page](security.md) apply, and the longer blockchain background is on [crypto fundamentals](crypto-fundamentals.md). The one-line definition is in the [glossary](glossary.md#solana).
