# Hedera primer

Hedera is a public distributed ledger. It does not build a chain of blocks in the usual way. It uses hashgraph, a gossip-based consensus algorithm that orders transactions by timestamps agreed among nodes. Its native coin is HBAR, which pays fees and secures the network through proof of stake. The total supply is fixed at 50 billion HBAR. This page is general information. It is not a view on the price and not a recommendation to hold HBAR.

The network is governed by the Hedera Council, a group of organisations with up to 39 seats and one vote each. That structure is the main study question: governance by a named council is a different trust model from open, permissionless validation. Fees are set in US-dollar terms and paid in HBAR. Keys follow the [security page](security.md), the directory card is on [chains](chains.md), and the glossary entry is [hashgraph](glossary.md#hashgraph).
