# HBAR primer

Hedera is a public network that orders transactions with hashgraph consensus: nodes gossip events, and the record of that gossip yields an order, rather than a single proof-of-work chain of blocks. HBAR is the token used for fees and for staking under Hedera's rules. This page is general information. It is not a view on the price and not a recommendation to hold HBAR.

Governance is published as a council model. Who sits on that council, and what they can change, is a fact you read in Hedera's own documents, because a directory line goes stale. Consensus design does not remove key risk. A signature still moves the tokens, and the [security page](security.md) applies. The glossary entries are [Hedera](glossary.md#hedera), [HBAR](glossary.md#hbar), and [hashgraph](glossary.md#hashgraph). The rest of the ledgers Atlas names are on the [chain directory](chains.md).
