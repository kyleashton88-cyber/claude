# Chainlink primer

Chainlink is an oracle network, not a separate layer-1 chain. An oracle carries information from outside a blockchain, such as a reference price, onto it so a smart contract can read it. Chainlink runs that work through independent node operators and aggregates their reports into feeds that many contracts on many chains read. Its token is LINK, issued on Ethereum, and it is used to pay node operators and in staking. This page is general information. It is not a view on the price and not a recommendation to hold LINK.

The study questions are about dependence. A lending market that reads one feed inherits that feed's update rules, its delay, and its operators. Cross-chain messaging adds a bridge-like seam. The oracle idea is on [crypto fundamentals](crypto-fundamentals.md), the wider directory is on [chains](chains.md), and the one-line definition is in the [glossary](glossary.md#oracle).
