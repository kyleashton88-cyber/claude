# LINK primer

Chainlink is an oracle network: independent node operators supply data, such as prices, that smart contracts can read. LINK is the token associated with that network. This page is general information. It is not a view on the price and not a recommendation to hold LINK.

A contract cannot see an off-chain price by itself. An oracle feed is the bridge for that fact, and the feed's sources, aggregation, and update rules belong to that feed, not to the brand name alone. A stale or thin feed is one of the ways a lending or derivatives contract can act on the wrong world. How oracles fail is in the [glossary](glossary.md#oracle). LINK's current role in paying or staking node operators is defined in the project's own documents and can change. Read those documents rather than a ticker page. The chain directory is [here](chains.md), and the wider ledger background is [crypto fundamentals](crypto-fundamentals.md).
