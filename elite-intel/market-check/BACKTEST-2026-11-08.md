# Gate check on the 8 Nov 2026 candidate set

Coins: BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, XLM, SHIB, ASTER.
Asked-for result: flag DOGE, XLM, SHIB and SOL. Keep BTC, ETH, XRP and ASTER.

The gate in `select.py` is the one that was run. drift30 is the 30-day close move divided by the 30-day high-low range (max high − min low). ADX is ADX(14) on those 30 daily bars. The example stop is the 90-day level (`5th percentile of low × 0.99 × 0.96`). Candles are Pionex public 1D klines fetched 15 Nov 2026. No separate research screen exists in this repo.

The published 8 Nov bottoms and stops match that 90-day formula on candles through 6 Nov UTC, which is the last closed daily at 6:48am Sydney on 8 Nov.
