Now the words this lesson needs, before any chart. Margin is the money you put up to hold a trade made with borrowed money. A contract here is a trade on the price made with borrowed money. You never hold the coin. A position is that open trade. Liquidation, in plain words, means an exchange closes a contract because the margin cannot carry it. A spot holding of coins is not that mechanism. A drop in the coin's price does not, by itself, close a spot holding for you. You still hold the coin. A contract might be closed by the exchange. That difference is the lesson.

The chart is not an exchange screen. It is the public path those days followed. Draw the losing path first. A later close drawn before that drop would tell the story backwards. Isolated and cross are two ways a borrowed trade can be boxed. Isolated means one bucket, and a loss stays in that bucket.
