# Public price windows

`btc-usd-windows.json` is our recreation of public daily Bitcoin prices in dollars.

- Source: Yahoo Finance chart endpoint, symbol BTC-USD, daily bars.
- Retrieved: 2026-11-06.
- Windows kept: 19 Jul 2023–16 Oct 2023, 1 Oct 2023–14 Mar 2024, 20 Feb 2020–23 Mar 2020, 27 Mar 2024–24 Jun 2024.
- Licence: public market prices, redrawn by this course. No exchange interface, logo, account, or balance is stored here.
- Use: teaching charts only. The path is stamped Example. It is not a forecast and not a result.

The July–October 2023 window is the house example. The public daily low was 24,930.30 and the public daily high was 30,330.64. The first daily close above that high was 23 Oct 2023, at 33,086. On 13 Mar 2020 the public daily low was 4,107.
