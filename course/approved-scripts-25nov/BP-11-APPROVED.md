A daily closing price is the last price of the day. This lesson uses the end of each UTC day, the world's standard clock, which is 11am Sydney time in daylight saving, 10am otherwise. When this lesson says inside or outside, it means that closing price.

A fill only swaps cash and coin. The tax office treats a swap like a sale, so a fill can belong in the journal. This is general information, not tax advice. Unrealised only means coin you still hold, priced at the latest closing price so you can see it change. It is not a tax figure. You can leave that row blank.

The stop is the sentence you write before you start. If a daily closing price finishes outside the range, and the next daily closing price is still outside, the example is over. On the way up, sells can fill and the page can sit in cash while the price rises. A fall through the bottom would leave you holding coin. You do not invent the stop after the closing price has left.
