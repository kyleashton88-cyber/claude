## Spoken script

GridBot Builder. GridBot Blueprint. Lesson six. Entry and exit rules.

Education only. Not financial advice. This is general information and doesn't consider your objectives, financial situation or needs. Crypto is high risk; you could lose all the money you put in. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. The prices are public historical daily prices, marked Example. The range uses the high and the low of that window. When we say inside or outside, we use the daily closing price. No order is sent from this lesson.

Entry without an exit is a page that cannot finish. The exit is a path you can point at. You write that path before you write the start. This lesson uses the shared Example. It does not choose a size, and it does not send an order. Every figure is marked Example. This is education only.

By the end you will write the finish rule before the start. You will point each sentence at a date on the chart. You will leave size off the page until both sentences exist.

A daily closing price is the last price of the day. This lesson uses the end of each UTC day, the world's standard clock, which is 11am Sydney time in daylight saving, 10am otherwise. A finish rule is the stop from the first lesson: the condition that ends the idea. The entry is the before sentence. It says the path you are looking at is still the range you measured. It is not a signal to start, and it is not, by itself, the day a bot starts. Position size is the ceiling you write, divided by the number of orders. For example only, a ceiling of 600 dollars across 6 orders is 100 dollars an order. That is arithmetic. It is not a size for your account, and it is not a result. You may leave the size line blank here. Nothing in this course tells you which ceiling to pick.

The shared Example range is 24,930 at the bottom and 30,331 at the top. The middle is 27,630. Six gaps of about 900 sit between the bottom and the top. You already counted those. This lesson does not recount them. It asks where the page ends.

Finish sentence, said once. If a daily closing price finishes outside the range and the next daily closing price is still outside, the summer idea is over. Spot stays spot in this lesson. Spot means you buy the coin with money you already have. Nothing here asks you to borrow, and nothing here asks you to multiply a size. There is no size to multiply. The page is two sentences and two dates.

The next closing price is part of the rule, so name it. On 24 October 2023 the Example closing price was 33,902. Size stays blank for a reason you can say. If you write a size before the finish sentence exists, the page can start and cannot end. The number would be sitting on a page with no exit. Leave it empty. This course leaves the size line blank on purpose. No later lesson picks the ceiling for you. This lesson only protects the order of the lines: finish, then before, then a blank size. Read them to someone else. If they cannot find 23 October from the finish sentence alone, the sentence is not done.
