# Public path used in Elite lessons

Retrieved 6 November 2026 from the public Yahoo Finance chart endpoint for the daily close of the large coin and of ether, 1 November 2021 through 1 December 2022. The files in this folder store an index rebased to 100 on the 8 November 2021 close. They do not store a dollar price, an exchange name, or a logo.

Figures the lessons are allowed to say, marked Example and not a forecast:

- The 21 November 2022 close was about 76 percent under the 8 November 2021 close.
- The 13 June 2022 close was 16 percent under the 12 June 2022 close. That day's high-to-low span was about 21 percent.
- Over the same window, daily log-return correlation of the two series was about 0.89. That figure is an example, not a rule. So is any older 0.8 tag.

US share trading paused on 9, 12, 16 and 18 March 2020. That is the breaker analogy. No venue logo is drawn.
