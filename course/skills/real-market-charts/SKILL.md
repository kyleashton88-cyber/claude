# Real market charts

`data/btc-index.csv` and `data/eth-index.csv` are daily indexes rebased to 100 on the 8 November 2021 close. `data/SOURCE.md` records where the closes came from and the three figures lessons may say.

`elite_motion.py` draws them in teal on navy:

- `chart-path` — one falling path, about 76 percent lower by 21 November 2022
- `chart-two` — bitcoin and ether on the same window, tag 0.89, Example, not a rule
- `chart-day` — 12 and 13 June 2022, the wide day, 16 percent on the close

No dollar sign, no exchange name, no logo. The picture is our drawing of public closes, not a screenshot.
