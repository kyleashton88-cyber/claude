# Instructional design for Elite

Each Elite lesson teaches one job, on paper, in teal. Blueprint cyan is not used.

1. Open on the problem the learner can point at.
2. Say the objective in one breath: "By the end you can..."
3. Teach the concepts in order. Every key concept is anchored to one real chart, one real event, or one real-life analogy, and that example is on screen while it is spoken.
4. Show one worked example. Mark it Example. It is not a forecast and not an instruction to trade.
5. Check understanding with a question whose answer is a shape on the page.
6. Recap in a few lines, then one action the learner writes, then the next lesson.

The real path in these lessons is the public index from 8 November 2021 to 21 November 2022. See `real-market-charts/SKILL.md`. Analogies (paper map, pre-flight, shelf, hand switch, two bridges, worksheet line, recipe card) carry the lessons that are not about a price path.

Do not name an exchange. Do not show a logo, a dollar sign, a personal balance, or a coin picture.
