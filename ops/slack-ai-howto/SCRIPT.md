# How to use AI with the Slack channel
Staff SOP · Market Pros · ~3:00 · 1920×1080 · education/ops (not public ads)

## Goal
Show a staff member the click-by-click path to ask company AI (Orchestrator / bots) in Slack, keep work in a thread, and read the reply.

## Example walkthrough
Channel: #all-audience-asset-co (C0BMXMP55T6)
Example ask (safe, staff-facing): "@Orchestrator Bot please summarise today's open staff to-dos in this channel."

## Beats (spoken ≈ 420–450 words @ ~145 wpm ≈ 3 min)
| # | Time | On-screen | VO |
|---|------|-----------|-----|
| 01 | 0:00–0:12 | Title card: How to use AI with Slack · Market Pros staff | Hook: In the next three minutes you'll ask our AI in Slack the right way — click by click. |
| 02 | 0:12–0:28 | Slack home / sidebar, highlight Channels | Open Slack. In the left sidebar, find Channels. |

| 07 | 1:55–2:20 | Thread opened on that message | Click Reply in thread. Put follow-ups, files, and clarifications in the thread — not a new top-level message. |
| 08 | 2:20–2:45 | Bot reply in thread | When the bot answers, read it in the thread. If something is wrong or missing, reply in the same thread. |
| 09 | 2:45–3:00 | Recap checklist card | Recap: open the right channel, @ the bot, one clear ask, keep it in a thread, read the reply. That's how we use AI with Slack. |
