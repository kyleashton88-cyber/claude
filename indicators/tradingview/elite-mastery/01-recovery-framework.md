# Recovery Framework: After a Range Break or Drawdown

> Education only, not financial advice. Grid bots can lose money. Only use money you can afford to lose.

**Fills gap:** "when to stop" and recovery at portfolio level. Suggested place: Grid Bot Elite Mastery → Elite Risk Management, after "Circuit Breakers & Kill Switches". Also suggested text for the empty chapter "Module 4: Recovery Framework (2 videos)" in the hidden course "Changing a Grid Bot in Choppy Conditions".

## The goal
Recovery is not about winning the money back fast. It's about stopping the damage, finding out what happened, and only redeploying when the plan makes sense again.

## Phase 1: Stop the bleeding (same day)
1. Check whether your circuit breaker or kill-switch rule has been hit. If yes, follow it. No exceptions.
2. Pause new orders on the affected layer or pair.
3. Check margin ratio and liquidation prices on every futures grid. Reduce exposure where they are close.
4. Don't add new capital today.

## Phase 2: Diagnose (within 48 hours)
Answer these in writing:
- What broke: range, leverage, sizing, correlation, news, or the platform?
- Did I follow my plan? If not, where did I go off it?
- Was the loss within my planned limit?

## Phase 3: Decide (one of four)
| Choice | When it fits |
|---|---|
| Stay out | Market is trending hard, or I broke my own rules |
| Re-range smaller | Market has settled into a new range |
| Move to spot only | I want to keep learning without liquidation risk |
| Reduce layers | Several layers were hit together (correlation) |

## Phase 4: Redeploy slowly
- Start with a smaller amount than before.
- Use the same or lower leverage. Never higher to "catch up".
- Write the new plan first, then launch.

## The rule that matters most
Never increase size or leverage to recover a loss. That is how a drawdown becomes an account wipe.
