# Records and Tax at Scale

> Education only. This is not tax, legal or financial advice. Speak to a registered tax agent or accountant.

**Fills gap:** tax awareness for Elite members (more bots, more exchanges, more records). Suggested place: Capital Scaling Strategies, last lesson.

## Why scale changes things
Several layers on several exchanges can create thousands of trades a year. Missing records are costly to rebuild later.

## Monthly routine (30 minutes)
1. Download trade history (CSV) from every exchange and sub-account.
2. Download deposit and withdrawal history.
3. Save to one folder per exchange per year.
4. Update your bot tracker: start and stop dates, settings, reasons.
5. Note any lost, hacked or frozen funds with dates and evidence.

## Talk to a professional about
- Whether your activity counts as investing or business in your country
- How futures, funding fees and fees are treated
- Record-keeping software that supports your exchanges

## Australian members
The ATO publishes guidance on crypto assets at ato.gov.au. A registered tax agent can apply it to your situation.
